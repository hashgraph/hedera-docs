## Overview

{/* Cover:
    - Coupons: periodic interest on a bond; today paid by a paying agent, on EVM chains by a keeper network (e.g. Chainlink Automation) or a bot that must be run and funded.
    - HIP-1215: a contract calls the Hedera Schedule Service system contract (0x16b) with scheduleCall(to, expirySecond, gasLimit, value, callData); the network itself calls back at that time. The contract reschedules itself each time: a self-perpetuating schedule.
    - "Only on Hedera": no keeper; the calling contract pays for the scheduled execution from its own HBAR.
    - What this template builds: a CouponBond (positions, coupon rate, period, number of coupons, maturity), deployed through a factory; coupons paid in an HTS stablecoin. */}

Actors:

* **Issuer**: deploys the factory once, issues coupon bonds, allocates positions to investors, funds the bond with stablecoin for coupons and principal, and with HBAR for its schedules
* **Investors**: demo investors, or your MetaMask account, who receive coupons and principal without doing anything
* **Hedera Schedule Service**: calls the bond at each coupon date

The lifecycle:

1. The Issuer issues a bond through the factory: face value, coupon rate per period, period length, number of coupons
2. The Issuer allocates positions and funds the bond
3. The Issuer starts the schedule: the bond schedules its first coupon with `scheduleCall`
4. At each coupon date the network calls the bond, which pays every holder and schedules the next coupon
5. At the last date it pays the final coupon and the principal, and schedules nothing more
