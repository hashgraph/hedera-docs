Issue a bond with an ISIN, a face value and a maturity date through Hedera's [Asset Tokenization Studio](/solutions/tokenization/ats/index) (ATS), approve the investors allowed to hold it, and distribute it to them. ATS deploys the bond for you from audited, upgradeable contracts already on testnet: you deploy nothing.

## Overview

{/* Cover:
    - What a tokenised bond is: a debt security with an ISIN, a face (nominal) value, a currency, a start and a maturity date, recorded as a token.
    - What ATS is: Hedera's open-source studio for digital securities; a factory deploys each bond as a Diamond (EIP-2535) proxy over shared, audited logic held by a Business Logic Resolver. ERC-1400 at the core.
    - Why "better on Hedera": the learner deploys nothing; one factory call yields a full security with roles, control lists, corporate-action support.
    - Relation to Asseto (commercial lifecycle platform from the same lineage).
    - Scope: issuance, allowlist, distribution. Coupons are the next workflow (HIP-1215); ERC-3643 identity is out of scope. */}

Three actors:

* **Issuer**: the operator account. Issues the bond through the factory and holds its issuer and control-list roles
* **Approved investor**: on the bond's allowlist; can receive and hold the bond
* **Unapproved investor**: not on the allowlist; the bond refuses to be issued or transferred to it

The lifecycle:

1. The Issuer resolves the bond configuration's latest version on the ATS resolver
2. The Issuer calls the factory's `deployBond` with the terms, the allowlist switched on, and its own roles
3. The Issuer approves investors by adding them to the bond's control list
4. The Issuer issues bonds to approved investors; issuing to an unapproved one is refused
5. A wallet investor (MetaMask) can hold bonds and transfer them, under the same allowlist

| Component | Technology |
| - | - |
| Bond | ATS v8 bond (ERC-1400), deployed by the testnet factory as a Diamond proxy |
| Issuance | Factory `deployBond`, signed by the Issuer over JSON-RPC (Hashio) |
| Allowlist | ATS control list in allowlist mode (`addToControlList`, `removeFromControlList`) |
| Distribution | `issue(holder, amount, data)`, guarded by the issuer role and the allowlist |
| Wallet investor | MetaMask via RainbowKit; ATS bonds are EVM contracts |
| Network | Hedera testnet |
