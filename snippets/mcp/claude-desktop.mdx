1. Go to **Customize → Connectors**, click **+ Add**, then select **Add custom connector**.
2. Enter the name `Hedera Docs` and the URL `https://docs.hedera.com/mcp`, then click **Continue**.
3. Under **Authentication**, choose **No sign-in**, because the Hedera docs server is public. Click **Continue**, then click **Add**.

**Verify:** Click the **"+"** button in the lower left of the chat box, select **Connectors**, and enable **Hedera Docs**.
